About

Me;

My name is Lalith Nayan, and I work in information security.

This blog is where I document research, security notes, CTFs, malware analysis, detection engineering, and whatever else I am actively trying to understand.

What will I write about?

I want this space to be useful without becoming another place that rehashes the same ideas for clicks.

I am keeping the plan simple: write about the domains I am researching, show the path I took, include the mistakes where they matter, and get a little sharper with each post.
